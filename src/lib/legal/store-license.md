## Publishing and royalties

### 4 Dividing composition income

For each Buyer release using the Beat, the Producer side and Buyer side each receive 50% of the composition income attributable to that release. The percentages total 100% of the composition side. The license price and master receipts are separate. Composition income includes writer and publisher performance income, mechanical royalties and the composition fee for sync, when received or credited to either side or its administrator.

Writers or their lawful assignees own their original new lyrics, melodies and other contributions. The economic split alone does not transfer ownership of those contributions. Income from unrelated works or from uses outside Buyer’s licensed release is excluded.

- **New songwriting.** The producer-side writers share 50% of the writer portion and the new writers share 50%. Corresponding publisher economics follow those percentages. Document the actual writers, publishers and any approved assignments before release as set out in § 7. On a newly written song, a buyer that contributed no authorship participates through its agreements with those entitled to the Buyer-side share.
- **Instrumental with no new songwriting.** The original writers remain the credited writers. The 50% Buyer-side entitlement is a contractual share of composition receipts from Buyer’s licensed release. Original writers and publishers register accurately and account for Buyer’s share. No one is to register Buyer as a writer merely for buying the Beat.

Each side pays its own collaborators, administrators and promised sample or contributor shares from its half. Count receipts paid directly to a side’s rights holders toward its half. Producer must obtain written authority for these economics and uses, including from anyone collecting directly. Do not list or release on these terms if an existing agreement prevents this division. Preserve legally nonwaivable payments and follow society rules for direct payment. Where direct splitting is unavailable, the receiving party accounts to the other side to the extent lawful.

### Sync fees and direct collections

Buyer may authorize permitted sync uses without fresh Producer approval on each placement. This authority is limited to the controlled rights in the rights schedule. Honor any required third-party approval expressly identified there for a use outside the offer. Allocate a combined, unitemized sync fee 50% to master and 50% to composition unless both parties agree otherwise in writing. Use good-faith, commercially reasonable allocations for separately priced rights.

Each side may administer its share. Within 45 days after a calendar quarter in which it collects for the other side, it sends a statement and pays the balance. Before splitting receipts, deduct only actual taxes withheld, genuine refunds and collection charges both sides approved in writing. Each side bears its own chosen commissions. Production and marketing costs cannot be deducted. Credit society payments once. Keep supporting records for three years and allow a reasonable annual inspection on notice, subject to confidentiality.
