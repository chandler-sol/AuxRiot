After successful payment and signed acceptance, Producer grants Buyer an exclusive, perpetual, worldwide license to the Beat's recording and underlying composition, to the extent Producer controls them. Producer retains copyright ownership. The license lasts for the full copyright term, subject to rights the law does not allow anyone to waive.

Buyer may edit, arrange, add vocals, create new recordings, reproduce, distribute, stream, sell, publicly perform and synchronize the Beat with video, advertising, films or games. Music videos and instrumental releases are allowed. There are no sales, stream, performance, territory or revenue caps.

Buyer may authorize collaborators, labels, distributors and sync customers as needed for these uses. Buyer may not resell or license the raw Beat or stems as another beat-store product, sample pack or stock-music asset.

Stems are included only when listed in the delivered files. A tagged file remains tagged; this agreement does not promise an untagged replacement.

## 3. Credit, publishing and royalties

Credit Producer as "Prod. by {producer_name}" in release metadata and accompanying credits wherever the service supports them.

For each song using the Beat, Producer retains 50% of the writer's share; Buyer and any other writers divide the remaining 50%. Associated publisher interests follow those songwriting percentages, with each side administering its own share unless separately agreed in writing. Producer handles collaborator splits within Producer's share. The parties must complete a split sheet and register consistently before release.

Producer receives no percentage of Buyer's master-recording revenue and no additional contractual master royalty. The one-time license payment does not buy out the publishing rights retained above.

## 4. What stays public

Producer must never sell, lease or license the Beat to anyone else. Producer may keep it in a portfolio or showreel, without downloadable files or another commercial license. AuxRiot may keep the completed battle and its playable two-minute preview with a "SOLD" stamp. These limited display and streaming permissions are reserved from the exclusive grant.

Only Buyer, or Buyer's authorized distributor, may register a resulting song with Content ID or a similar system, if eligible. Buyer must exclude or release claims against these authorized previews and portfolio uses. Producer must remove conflicting registrations.

AuxRiot does not clear samples. Unavailable promised rights make the Beat not as described.
