## Disputes and seller recovery

### 8 Who funds refunds and chargebacks

AuxRiot initiates refunds promised here even if the producer has already received a payout. We coordinate the processor reversal, return our fee on a reversed sale, adjust refundable tax and credit sums already recovered. We send the seller an itemized notice showing the reason, credits and balance. The following limits apply.

- **Ordinary reversal or seller-caused defect.** The seller returns the net proceeds received, or gives up unpaid proceeds. The seller bears actual, unreimbursed processing costs. A documented, material seller breach, including a missing promised clearance, also makes the seller responsible for reasonable, directly attributable, unreimbursed dispute fees. AuxRiot credits its fee and all processor or tax recoveries. We reconcile any promotion we funded so recovery does not exceed the loss.
- **Disputed copyright claim.** A notice alone does not establish a seller breach. While unresolved, the reversed sale is unwound at net proceeds and AuxRiot bears unrecovered processing costs. We give the seller the evidence we can lawfully share, normally ten business days to respond before a final fault allocation, and a written decision. If a fulfilled sale was unwound solely because of a demonstrably mistaken claim, we restore the innocent seller’s net and bear the remaining costs. A putback by itself does not establish ownership.
- **Payment fraud or platform error.** AuxRiot bears unrecovered processor and dispute costs for unauthorized-card fraud or our own error absent seller involvement or breach. On a reversed, fulfilled sale caused solely by our error, we restore the innocent seller’s net. Otherwise a reversed transaction returns the parties’ sale proceeds as above. Where a dispute is won, we promptly credit recovered sums to the party that bore the loss.

Banks set card-dispute deadlines. We may temporarily hold the disputed amount while seeking evidence and release it when the reason ends. A provisional chargeback alone does not terminate the license. A final full reversal has the effect of a full refund. Seller authorizes reversals, offsets against eligible proceeds, or direct repayment only as the law and Stripe agreements allow. We obtain any required bank-debit consent. Pay any unrecovered, undisputed balance within 30 days of the itemized notice. Email hello@auxriot.com to dispute an amount. We pause direct collection of the disputed part during review. Any amount upheld is due 30 days after our written decision. No double recovery.
