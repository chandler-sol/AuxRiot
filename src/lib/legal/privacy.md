# AuxRiot Privacy Policy

*Information we use to run battles, process store orders and protect accounts*

Version 5 · Last updated November 27, 2026

### 1 Who handles your data

Chandler Solomon operates AuxRiot at https://auxriot.com in Waxhaw, North Carolina, USA. For access, correction, deletion, export or a privacy concern, email hello@auxriot.com. This policy applies to visitors and account holders, including those buying or selling beats.

### 2 What we collect

- **Accounts.** Your username, email, account settings, age affirmation and acceptance records. We protect passwords with salted, one-way hashes. If you choose Google or Discord sign-in, we receive your provider account ID, email, email-verification status and display name. We do not receive your provider password.
- **Content and activity.** Public profiles, avatars, bios, social links, signature colors, battle audio, votes, scores, listening progress, comments, upvotes, reports, results, respect, flair and Credit activity. Battle previews are trimmed and converted to MP3 in your browser. Private store delivery files are handled separately.
- **Store transactions.** Legal and public credit names, entity and signatory capacity, listing details, private files, package and tag status, contributor and sample disclosures, clearance evidence, prices, currency, tax and any exemption evidence. We record contract versions, electronic signatures and times, identifiers for the order and files, payment and payout status, delivery events, refunds, dispute materials and support messages.
- **Payment onboarding.** Stripe collects payment credentials and, according to the account and country, identity, banking and tax details. AuxRiot receives the identifiers, verification status, transaction or payout information and other details Stripe makes available for platform responsibilities. Provide sensitive verification documents directly through the designated secure flow. Do not submit card, bank or identity documents in private messages.
- **Communications.** Public listing questions and answers, private messages, customer support, moderation and copyright reports, removal requests, privacy requests, battle-alert preferences and newsletter opt-in records.

### Security and listening signals

We record listening progress to apply the 75% voting rule. Our anti-fraud records contain salted, one-way hashes of IP addresses and random device identifiers. A cookie holds a random device identifier for fraud checks. Section 5 explains its use for counting views. Our systems and Cloudflare receive IP addresses before hashing. Cloudflare Turnstile processes browser, device and connection signals on signup. Hashed identifiers can still be personal data.

## How information is used and shared

### 3 Why we use information

We use account and security data to authenticate users, recover accounts, detect linked accounts, rate-limit login and signup, investigate abuse and keep battles fair. We use content and activity to stream battles, count listening, run votes, rankings and Credits, send selected alerts, operate profiles and understand site performance.

We use transaction data to verify sellers, match the correct parties, generate signed licenses, calculate prices and tax, process payments and payouts, privately deliver files, address refunds and chargebacks and meet reporting duties. We use submitted reports and communications to support users, handle appeals, enforce rules and respond to legal obligations.

Checks compare voting patterns with device or IP signals. They may discount votes or limit comment visibility or account access. Shared signals alone do not establish wrongdoing. Ask for human review at hello@auxriot.com. Providing required account, transaction and verification information lets us perform the requested service. Bios, avatars, social links and newsletter subscriptions are optional.

### 4 What other people can see

Your public profile, battle previews, completed results, comments, store listings and public listing questions and answers can be copied, indexed or shared by others. Scores stay hidden until voting ends. Your account email, private purchase files, signatures and payout records are not public profile fields. Ordinary moderation tools do not show your account email. An address you put in a profile, post or message is visible to whoever can see that content.

The producer and buyer receive the legal identities, public credit name, signature records and transaction details needed for their shared agreement. We may provide relevant clearance evidence in redacted form. The shared license excludes tax IDs, banking details and ID-verification documents. We do not publish the signed agreement. Use a public credit name if you want release credits to differ from your legal name.

Private messages are visible to their participants. Automated checks flag phone numbers, email addresses and payment-app references to warn about off-site deals. Moderators may read a conversation when a participant reports a message. Authorized administrators and providers may also access necessary data to deliver the service, handle a legal request or address a documented security incident. Private messages are not public listings.

We show the automated-check and report-access notice before a person sends a private store message.

### 5 Cookies and analytics

We use login-session, device-ID and short-lived Google or Discord sign-in cookies. The device ID helps prevent fraud. Where permission is required to use it to count views, that use stays off until you agree. You can decline or withdraw that permission through the same cookie controls and still use the security features. Cloudflare Web Analytics provides aggregate site statistics without analytics cookies or cross-site visitor tracking. Turnstile, payment pages and ongoing network delivery process separate data. AuxRiot does not set advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. Where required, we ask before optional cookies or device uses start. Blocking optional cookies may affect the related features.

## Providers retention and security

### 6 Email choices

Resend delivers account confirmations, password resets, transaction and service notices and selected battle alerts. Turn battle alerts off in settings or use their unsubscribe link. The AuxRiot news newsletter requires separate opt-in. You can stop marketing emails using the unsubscribe link. We apply that choice across our mailing lists and keep a record so you stay unsubscribed. Necessary security, order and legal messages continue. We keep promotions out of those messages.

### 7 Who receives information

- Cloudflare provides hosting, database, file storage, Web Analytics and Turnstile. Resend provides email delivery and subscription handling. Google and Discord provide sign-in if selected.
- Stripe and its payment partners process payments, onboarding, verification, payouts, disputes and applicable tax reporting. Banks and card networks may receive required information. Stripe’s privacy policy is available at https://stripe.com/privacy. Stripe may use information for its own compliance, fraud prevention and payment-network responsibilities.
- Buyers and producers receive the order information described in § 4. Authorized advisers, authorities and parties to a legal or copyright claim may receive information reasonably needed to meet lawful obligations, resolve disputes or protect rights. We tell people submitting copyright notices what will be forwarded. We forward the required notice and contact details and redact unrelated personal information. An informal complaint alone does not entitle anyone to private account records.

We do not sell or rent personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising or targeted advertising.

### 8 How long we keep it

We keep account data while the account exists. Public content and completed battles normally stay until removed. Anti-fraud hashes and ordinary security logs are retained for up to 12 months from collection, subject to a limited, documented legal or dispute hold. Nonpublic backups rotate within 30 days. We reapply deletions if a backup is restored.

When an account is deleted, we remove personal profile data or irreversibly anonymize eligible records. We retain only the signed license, necessary chain-of-rights, order and delivery evidence for as long as needed to evidence or administer outstanding rights. Tax, payout and refund records are kept for required accounting periods and dispute windows. We may retain claim or safety records while a matter or legal obligation requires them. We restrict access, review retention and delete information when the reason ends. Request a copy of your signed agreement by email, including after account closure.

### 9 Security and international processing

We protect data in transit, secure stored passwords and session tokens, and limit staff access. If a security incident requires notice, we notify affected people and the relevant authorities as the law requires. Data may be processed in the United States or other countries. Where transfer rules apply, we use the applicable adequacy decision, approved contractual safeguards or another lawful transfer mechanism. Contact us for information about safeguards that apply to your data and a copy where available.

## Your privacy rights

### 10 Requests and choices

Email hello@auxriot.com to request access, correction, deletion, an export or review of a decision. We ask only for what we reasonably need to verify your identity, protect other people’s information and keep records where a legal exception applies. We explain denials and any available appeal. From regions that provide an appeal, ask for reconsideration at the same address. We will explain any available regulator complaint route.

### European Union, EEA and United Kingdom

Where applicable, we process the account, battle and purchase information needed to perform our contracts, and tax and regulatory records to meet legal obligations. We rely on legitimate interests for security, fraud prevention, service improvement and the handling of claims, balanced against your rights. We use consent for optional marketing and for cookies or device uses that require it.

Where EU or UK data protection law applies, you have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict or object to processing, receive portable data and request human review, subject to applicable conditions. You can withdraw consent and object to direct marketing at any time. We stop using your data for that marketing when you object. You may complain to your local supervisory authority, including the UK Information Commissioner. We normally respond within one month. If we need a permitted extension, we tell you why within that first month. You may request the information described in § 9 about international transfers.

### California

If the California Consumer Privacy Act applies, residents may request to know, access, correct or delete personal information, receive a portable copy and exercise rights without discrimination. An authorized agent may submit a request subject to verification. The data described here include identifiers, customer and commercial records, online activity, audio or visual content, account credentials and fraud-related inferences. Sections 2-9 describe sources, purposes, recipients and retention.

We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising as California law defines those terms. We use account credentials, payment or verification information for the requested service, security and legal duties. For covered requests to know, correct or delete, we acknowledge receipt within 10 business days and normally respond within 45 calendar days. We explain any legally permitted extension of up to 45 more days.

### 11 Age and policy changes

AuxRiot accounts are for adults aged 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from under-18s. Email hello@auxriot.com if you believe a minor has an account, and we will investigate, restrict access and remove associated personal information as appropriate. We will post updates and notify users before material changes take effect. Where a new use requires consent, we obtain it before starting that use.
