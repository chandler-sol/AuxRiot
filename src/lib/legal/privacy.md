# AuxRiot Privacy Policy

## The short version

- Your profile, beats, battles and comments are public. Your email is not public or shown to moderators.
- We use account, activity and security data to run battles and fight cheating.
- We store passwords as salted hashes, and store hashed IP and device identifiers in our anti-fraud records. Hashing does not necessarily make data anonymous.
- We use login, sign-in and device cookies, plus cookieless Cloudflare Web Analytics. No advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.
- We do not sell or rent personal data or share it for targeted advertising. Our news newsletter is opt-in.
- Email [hello@auxriot.com](mailto:hello@auxriot.com) for access, correction, deletion or an export.

## 1. Who handles your data

AuxRiot, at https://auxriot.com, is operated by Chandler Solomon, based in Waxhaw, North Carolina, USA. We are responsible for the personal data described here. Contact: [hello@auxriot.com](mailto:hello@auxriot.com).

## 2. What we collect

**Account details.** Your username, email and account settings. If you use a password, we store only a salted PBKDF2 hash, not your readable password.

**Optional social sign-in.** If you choose Google or Discord, we receive your provider account ID, email, email-verification status and display name. We do not receive your provider password.

**Content and activity.** Uploaded audio, avatars, bios, social links, signature colors, comments, votes and scores, upvotes, reports, records, respect, flair and Credit activity. Beats are trimmed and converted to MP3 in your browser before upload.

**Store and messages.** Beats you list for sale (prices, store details and the private files buyers receive), public questions and answers on listings, and private messages between buyers and producers. Private messages are seen only by the two people in the conversation. Moderators can read a conversation only if someone in it reports a message. We flag messages that mention phone numbers, email addresses or payment apps so we can warn about deals outside AuxRiot, which aren't protected.

**Listening and views.** We record listening progress per beat to enforce the 75% listening rule. Beat view counts use a hashed device identifier. These are separate from Cloudflare Web Analytics.

**Security signals.** Our anti-fraud records store salted, one-way hashes of IP addresses and random device identifiers. We use them, with voting patterns, to detect linked accounts, prevent vote rigging and rate-limit login and signup attempts. The random identifier also exists in a cookie on your device.

Our systems and Cloudflare receive your IP address before hashing. Cloudflare Turnstile processes browser, device and connection signals on signup to distinguish people from bots. Hashed identifiers may still be personal data.

**Communications.** Information you send us in support requests, reports, privacy requests or copyright notices, plus newsletter choices and the time you opted in.

## 3. Why we use it

We use this information to:

- Create accounts, authenticate logins and recover access.
- Host and stream beats, run votes, publish results and manage Credits and profiles.
- Moderate content, investigate reports, prevent abuse and protect accounts.
- Send account messages and your chosen battle alerts or newsletter.
- Understand site performance, answer requests and meet legal obligations.

Required account and security information lets us create and secure your account. Bios, avatars, social links and newsletter subscriptions are optional.

Automated anti-cheat checks compare shared device or IP hashes and voting patterns. They may discount votes or restrict comment visibility or account access. Shared signals are not conclusive proof of wrongdoing. Contact [hello@auxriot.com](mailto:hello@auxriot.com) to challenge a restriction or request human review.

## 4. What is public

Your username, avatar, bio, social links, signature color, win/loss record, battles, beats, comments, store listings, public listing questions and answers, and flair are public. Private messages are not public. Battle scores stay hidden until voting ends. Public content may be copied, indexed or shared by others.

Your email is never shown publicly or to moderators. Authorized administrators and providers may access it to run the service. Reports and security details are not public profile fields.

## 5. Cookies and analytics

We use a login session cookie, a device-ID cookie for fraud prevention and view counting, and short-lived Google/Discord sign-in cookies. We do not set advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. Blocking cookies may prevent features from working.

Cloudflare Web Analytics provides aggregate traffic and performance statistics without analytics cookies or cross-site visitor tracking. Cloudflare states it does not collect or use visitors' personal data. Connection handling and Turnstile security processing are separate.

## 6. Email choices

Resend delivers account confirmations, password resets and battle alerts about opponents answering, results and waiting challenges. Turn battle alerts off in settings or use their one-click unsubscribe link.

The optional "AuxRiot news" newsletter requires a separate opt-in. We record when you opt in, and you can unsubscribe at any time. Unsubscribing from optional email does not stop necessary account, security or legal messages. We may keep a minimal unsubscribe record to respect your choice.

## 7. Who receives information

We use:

- **Cloudflare:** hosting, database, file storage, Web Analytics and Turnstile bot protection.
- **Resend:** email delivery and subscription handling.
- **Google or Discord:** authentication only if you choose that sign-in method.

Providers receive information needed for their tasks. Google and Discord also follow their own privacy policies.

We may share information when legally required, to handle copyright claims, or as reasonably necessary to protect rights and safety. Copyright notices and counter-notices may be forwarded to the other party, including contact information required for the process.

We do not sell or rent personal data, share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, or use it for targeted advertising.

## 8. How long we keep it

We keep account data while your account exists. Uploaded content and completed battles normally stay available until removed. Anti-fraud hashes and security logs are kept for up to 12 months after collection.

On deletion, we remove account details and personal content or irreversibly anonymize retained records. We may keep genuinely anonymous battle records and votes so past results remain accurate. Merely replacing a username with an identifier is not necessarily anonymization.

We may retain limited, protected information as necessary for legal duties, disputes, copyright claims or documented fraud prevention, deleting it when no longer needed. Nonpublic backups are overwritten within 30 days. If restored, deletion requests must be reapplied.

## 9. Security and international processing

We use HTTPS, salted PBKDF2 password hashes and hashed server-side session tokens. Access is limited to people and providers who need it. No system is completely secure.

We are based in the USA, and providers may process information in other countries with different privacy laws.

## 10. Your choices and rights

Email [hello@auxriot.com](mailto:hello@auxriot.com) for access, correction, deletion, an export or privacy concerns. We may verify your identity and protect others' information. We explain any lawful reason we cannot fulfill a request.

### EU, EEA and UK

Where GDPR or UK GDPR applies, our legal bases are contract necessity for accounts and battles; legitimate interests in security, fraud prevention and service improvement; consent for the optional newsletter and any cookies requiring consent; and legal obligations where applicable.

You may have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict or object to processing, and receive portable data. You can withdraw consent without affecting earlier lawful processing. You may complain to your local supervisory authority, including the UK ICO. We normally respond within one month and explain any permitted extension.

### California

If CCPA/CPRA applies to us, California residents can request to know, access, correct or delete personal information, subject to legal exceptions, and exercise rights without discrimination. Authorized agents may submit requests with appropriate verification.

The categories described above include identifiers, account records, internet activity, audio/visual content and fraud-related inferences. Their sources, purposes, recipients and retention are explained above. We do not sell or share data for targeted advertising, so there is no such activity to opt out of. We use account credentials only for necessary account and security functions.

For covered requests to know, correct or delete, we acknowledge receipt within 10 business days and respond within 45 calendar days, with a further 45 days if legally permitted and explained.

## 11. Age and policy changes

AuxRiot is for people 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect personal data from under-18s. Tell us if an underage person has an account so we can investigate and remove it and associated personal data as appropriate.

We will post updates here and give notice of material changes before they take effect. Where legally required, we will obtain consent before starting a new use.
