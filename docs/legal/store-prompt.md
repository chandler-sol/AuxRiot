## How the store works (facts to build on)

- **Exclusive sales only.** No leases. Each beat sells once to one buyer, then it's permanently off the market. The battle it competed in stays on the site with a "SOLD" stamp, and the 2-minute battle preview stays playable.
- **What the buyer gets:** a full-length audio file the producer uploads privately. The producer chooses WAV or MP3, tagged or untagged; buyers are told which before buying. Optionally, a zip of stems, for a higher "Exclusive + Stems" price.
- **Buying unlocks after the battle's vote ends**, so purchases can't influence voting.
- **Producers set their own prices.** We suggest at least $20 but allow lower.
- **AuxRiot's fee:** 20% of the final sale price. It may change for future sales with notice.
- **Card processing** (about 2.9% + 30¢ per sale) comes out of the producer's share, shown to them before they list.
- **Payments:** processed by Stripe. Producers are paid through Stripe Connect; AuxRiot never holds producers' money. Payouts go out on a regular schedule (currently weekly). Producers must complete Stripe's identity and tax verification to be paid.
- **Sales tax:** collected where required.
- **Refunds:** available only before the buyer downloads the files. After download, sales are final, except where law requires otherwise or the files are defective or not as described.
- **Discount codes:**
  - Producers can create codes for their own beats; the discount comes off the sale price, and our 20% applies to the discounted price.
  - AuxRiot runs occasional site-wide sales; for those, the producer is still paid on the full price and AuxRiot covers the discount.
- **Downloads:** buyers must have an account. Download links last 7 days, and purchases can be re-downloaded from their account.
- **License certificate:** each sale generates a PDF with the buyer's and producer's names, the beat title, date, price and order number.
- **One seller per beat.** Whoever lists it is responsible for it and gets paid. We don't handle co-producer splits; the seller must have their collaborators' permission and settle splits themselves.
- **Samples:** sellers confirm the beat has never been sold or leased anywhere else. Clearing any samples is the responsibility of the seller and the buyer, not AuxRiot. We only remove beats on a valid DMCA notice. If a beat is removed after a sale because of a valid copyright claim, [the buyer gets a refund from AuxRiot and it's recovered from the producer / the buyer's remedy is against the producer only].
- **Messaging:** buyers can ask public questions on a listing and send private messages to producers. Moderators review a private conversation only if someone reports a message. [We prohibit arranging payment outside AuxRiot for beats listed on AuxRiot / We strongly discourage it but don't prohibit it]; off-site deals aren't protected.
- **Battle Credits:** our in-site points have no cash value and can't be used toward purchases.
