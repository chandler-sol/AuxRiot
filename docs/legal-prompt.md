Write two documents for my website, a **Terms of Service** and a **Privacy Policy**, in plain, friendly English (short sections, clear headings, no walls of legalese). Output each as Markdown. Flag anything you think a lawyer should double-check with "⚠️ Lawyer check:".

**About the site**
- Name: AuxRiot, at https://auxriot.com. Operated by [your full name or business name], based in [city, state], USA. Contact email: [contact email].
- What it is: 1v1 beat battles for music producers. A producer uploads an instrumental beat (max 2 minutes; it's trimmed and converted to MP3 in their browser before upload) and picks a 24- or 48-hour vote. The first producer to answer with their own beat takes the other slot. Logged-in voters must listen to 75% of both beats, then score Drums, Melody, Mix and Overall from 1 to 5. Scores stay hidden until voting closes; the winner goes on both producers' records.
- Beats are **streamed only**. There are no downloads.
- Comments on battles, with upvotes. Upvotes earn a "respect" score that unlocks profile flair.
- User profiles: username, avatar image, bio, social links, signature color, win/loss record.
- **Battle Credits**: earned by finishing battles (win/tie/loss), sometimes given by admins in giveaways. They can only be spent on promoting your battle to the top of the feed for a few hours. Credits have **no cash value**, can't be bought (yet), sold, transferred or cashed out, and can be removed for cheating. We may change earn rates or prices. (Paid credit packs may come later. Write the terms so buying could be added, but say it's not offered now.)
- No gambling, betting or prizes of real-world value.
- Moderation: moderators and admins can hide comments, discount or remove votes, remove beats or battles, reverse results and credits, shadowban (silently discount a user's votes and hide their comments from others) and suspend accounts (can't log in). We use automated anti-cheat checks for alt accounts and vote rigging (shared device or IP, voting patterns). Users can report battles and comments.
- Copyright: users promise they own or have cleared everything in their beats (no uncleared samples). We follow the DMCA notice-and-takedown process via our form at https://auxriot.com/dmca, and remove beats on valid notices. Include a repeat-infringer policy and a counter-notice section. Our designated DMCA agent is [name / address / email, the same one you register with the US Copyright Office].
- License: users keep ownership of their beats. They grant us a license to host, stream, display and promote them on AuxRiot and in social share images or links, for as long as the content is on the site (plus a reasonable period for backups). Battles that have finished stay on the site as part of the record unless removed.
- Age: users must be at least [13 / 16 / 18 — pick one] years old.
- We may change features or end the service; accounts can be deleted on request by emailing [contact email].

**Data we collect (for the Privacy Policy)**
- Account: username, email address, password (stored only as a salted hash), or a Google/Discord sign-in (we receive their account ID, email, whether the email is verified and display name; we don't get their password).
- Content: uploaded audio, avatar images, bio, social links, comments, votes and scores, upvotes, reports.
- Anti-fraud signals: IP address and a random device cookie, stored **only as salted one-way hashes**, used to detect duplicate accounts and vote rigging, and to rate-limit login and sign-up attempts.
- Listening progress per beat (to enforce the 75% rule). View counts use a hashed device ID.
- Cookies: a login session cookie, a device-ID cookie (anti-fraud), and short-lived cookies during Google/Discord sign-in. **No advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.**
- Analytics: Cloudflare Web Analytics, which is cookieless and doesn't collect personal data.
- Bot protection: Cloudflare Turnstile on sign-up.
- Email: sent through Resend. Account emails (confirm email, password reset) and battle alerts (someone answered your battle, results, a reminder if your battle is waiting). Battle alerts can be turned off in settings or with the one-click unsubscribe link. Optional "AuxRiot news" newsletter is opt-in only; we store when they opted in.
- Service providers: Cloudflare (hosting, database, file storage, analytics, bot protection), Resend (email), Google and Discord (only if the user chooses to sign in with them). We don't sell or rent personal data and don't share it for advertising.
- Public by design: username, avatar, bio, social links, record, battles, beats, comments and flair. Email is never shown publicly, not even to moderators.
- Retention: account data is kept while the account exists. On deletion we remove personal data but may keep anonymized battle records and votes so past results stay accurate. Anti-fraud hashes and logs are kept [e.g. up to 12 months].
- User rights: access, correction, deletion and data export by emailing [contact email]. Include short GDPR (EU/UK) and CCPA/CPRA (California) sections in plain English. We don't sell data or use it for targeted advertising.
- Security: passwords hashed (PBKDF2), sessions stored hashed, HTTPS everywhere.
